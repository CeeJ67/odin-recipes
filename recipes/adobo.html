Adobo

Description

A classic Filipino dish made with meat, vinegar, soy sauce, garlic, and pepper.

Ingredients

  • Chicken or pork
  • Soy sauce
  • Vinegar
  • Garlic
  • Bay leaves
  • Black pepper
  • Water

Steps

  1. Combine the meat, soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, bay leaves, and pepper.
  2. Bring to a boil and simmer until the meat is tender.
  3. Add water if needed and continue simmering.
  4. Serve with rice.
