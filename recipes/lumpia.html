Lumpia

Description

Crispy Lumpia filled with seasoned ground meat and vegetables.

Ingredients

  • Ground pork
  • Carrots
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Lumpia wrappers
  • Salt and pepper
  • Cooking oil

Steps

  1. Mix the pork, vegetables, garlic, salt, and pepper.
  2. Wrap the mixture in lumpia wrappers.
  3. Heat oil and fry the lumpia until golden and crispy.
  4. Drain and serve.
