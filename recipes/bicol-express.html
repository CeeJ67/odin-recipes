Bicol Express

Description

A spicy and flavorful dish made with pork, vegetables, and a rich, savory sauce.

Ingredients

  • Ground pork
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Tomatoes
  • Salt and pepper
  • Cooking oil

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a pan and sauté garlic and onion until fragrant.
  2. Add ground pork and cook until browned.
  3. Add tomatoes, salt, and pepper. Cook until the sauce thickens.
  4. Serve hot with rice.
